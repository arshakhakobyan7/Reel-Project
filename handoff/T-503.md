## What to build
1. `make_thumbnail(ffmpeg, ffprobe, video, title, target, *, subtitle="", font=..., skip_seconds=180,
   on_cancel=None) -> ThumbnailResult` (path, size in bytes, the frame time used, JPEG quality).
   - Representative frame with FFmpeg's `thumbnail` filter, skipping the first `skip_seconds`
     (less when the video is shorter; a very short video uses its middle).
   - Output exactly 1280×720 JPEG (scale and pad/crop so the aspect ratio is kept), the title
     drawn with `drawtext` and the bold font: wrapped to at most 3 lines, readable over any picture
     (e.g. a semi-transparent box), optional smaller subtitle (e.g. "Python 12 · Դաս 14").
   - Under 2 MB: start at a high quality and lower it step by step until it fits; fail clearly if
     it never fits. Write atomically (`.part`, then rename).
   - Escape everything `drawtext` treats specially (`:`, `'`, `\`, `%`, `,` …) — titles come from
     the LLM and staff. Pass text through a UTF-8 file (`textfile=`) if that is simpler and safer.
2. **Glyph coverage check without new dependencies:** read the font's `cmap` table with
   `struct` (formats 4 and 12) and report characters of the title the font cannot draw.
   `make_thumbnail` refuses (clear error listing them) instead of drawing empty boxes.
3. Long Armenian titles: wrapping counts characters, not bytes.

## Acceptance criteria (from TASKS.md) — each needs a test
- size 1280×720, JPEG format, under 2 MB (check with `probe` and the file size);
- Armenian text renders without missing-glyph boxes: the cmap check passes for the Armenian
  alphabet (Ա–Ֆ, ա–և, punctuation ։ ՝ ՞) and Latin; a title with a character the font lacks
  (e.g. an emoji or CJK) is refused;
- plus: the frame is taken after `skip_seconds`; special characters in titles are drawn, not
  interpreted; 3-line wrapping; cancellation stops FFmpeg.
Keep integration media short (≤ 30 s, 320×240 source is fine; output is still 1280×720).
