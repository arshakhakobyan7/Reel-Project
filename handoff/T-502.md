## What to build
1. `Chapter(start: float, title: str)` and `format_timestamp(seconds, *, long: bool) -> str`:
   `M:SS` (e.g. `0:00`, `9:05`, `59:59`) or `H:MM:SS` (`1:02:03`); `long=True` for videos of an
   hour or more, so all chapters of one video use the same format. Seconds are floored.
2. `check_chapters(chapters, duration, settings) -> list[Chapter]` with YouTube's rules: first at
   0:00 (a first chapter within the first `min_chapter_seconds` is moved to 0), ascending, inside
   the video, at least `min_chapters`, each at least `min_chapter_seconds` long (also the last one,
   until the end of the video), titles stripped, non-empty, one line, ≤ `max_chapter_title_chars`
   (cut at a word boundary with `…`), no `<` `>`. Raises `ChapterError` with a clear reason when the
   list cannot be made valid (T-501 then retries or falls back to no chapters).
3. `render_title(settings, *, group, lesson, date, topic="") -> str` from `title_template`
   (`TITLE_FIELDS`); ≤ 100 characters (cut at a word boundary with `…`), no `<` `>`, one line,
   no doubled spaces.
4. `build_description(*, summary, chapters, duration, footer_fields, settings) -> str`:
   summary paragraph, blank line, the chapter list (`0:00 Title` per line; omitted when there are
   no chapters), then the rendered `description_footer` (`FOOTER_FIELDS`) if set. ≤ 5000 **bytes**
   UTF-8 (Armenian letters are 2 bytes): when too long, shorten the summary first, never break
   the chapter list (if the chapters alone do not fit, drop the chapter list and say so through a
   returned flag or a separate result type — your choice, document it). No `<` `>`.
